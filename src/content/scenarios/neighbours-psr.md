If someone relies on electricity for medical equipment, or needs help to leave home, preparation is mostly **services and contacts**, not gadgets.

## Priority Services Register

In England, Scotland and Wales, ask your gas, electricity and water suppliers about the Priority Services Register so they know you may need extra support. In Northern Ireland these are typically called Customer Care Registers—confirm with your supplier.

## Paper plans and telecare

Keep a written medication list and device instructions. Ask telecare or device providers how equipment behaves in a power cut. Landline-dependent alarms may need a backup solution from the phone provider.

## Neighbours

When it is safe, check on people who may need help. Swap phone numbers in advance. Share factual updates from official sources only.
