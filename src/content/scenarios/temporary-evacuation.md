A grab bag is for **temporary** leave when authorities advise it—not an off-grid lifestyle. If there is a **fire**, don’t waste time rescuing valuables: get out, stay out and call 999.

## What belongs in a leave-home bag

- Paper household plan and key phone numbers
- Medication, prescriptions and glasses
- Phone charger or power bank
- Small water and snacks
- Copies of important documents in a waterproof pouch
- Spare keys; a little cash
- Compact first-aid items
- Baby or pet supplies if needed

Agree a meeting point if you cannot return home. Add ICE or Medical ID on phones and tell the nominated contact. Keep the bag somewhere you can find it, and keep exits clear.
