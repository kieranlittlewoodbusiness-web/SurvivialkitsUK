A grab bag is for **temporary** leave when authorities advise it—not an off-grid lifestyle. If you are escaping a **fire**, take nothing: get out, stay out, call 999.

## What belongs in a leave-home bag

- Paper household plan and key phone numbers
- Medication, prescriptions and glasses
- Phone charger or power bank
- Small water and snacks
- Copies of important documents in a waterproof pouch
- Spare keys; a little cash
- Compact first-aid items
- Baby or pet supplies if needed

Agree a meeting point if you cannot return home. Add ICE or Medical ID on phones and tell the nominated contact. Keep the bag findable in the dark without blocking fire exits.
