Official UK resilience messaging is clear: **see what you already have**, then build gradually on ordinary shops. You do not need a speciality survival store.

## Inventory before shopping

Open the cupboard and fridge store. Note tins, dried fruit, nuts, crackers, long-life milk alternatives, and anything your household already eats that needs no fridge or cooking. Check you have a tin opener. Include baby formula (ready-to-feed is safer if you cannot boil water) and pet food if needed.

## Quantities that match guidance

Plan for about **three days**. Official checklists emphasise non-perishable food you would usually eat. Pair food planning with drinking water of about **3 litres per person per day** for drinking, plus more for hygiene and cooking if required—confirm the current wording on the Be Ready checklist when you review.

## Spread the cost

Pick up one or two missing items on normal trips. Set a six-month reminder to rotate stock and check dates. This is household logistics, not stockpiling for an imagined deadline.
