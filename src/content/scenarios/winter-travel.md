Plan ahead and avoid travel unless essential when severe weather is forecast. Check national traffic services and Met Office warnings before you set off.

## Boot kit checklist

Ready Scotland and Be Ready guidance commonly include:

- Torch and spare batteries
- Warm clothes, boots and a blanket
- High-visibility jacket
- Ice scraper and de-icer
- Phone charger
- Battery jump leads
- Compact shovel, map, snacks and a warm drink in a flask
- First-aid kit

Inventory coats, scrapers and torches you already own first. Then use [winter travel criteria](/gear/winter-travel/)—retailer links come after the guidance.
