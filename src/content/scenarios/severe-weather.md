A weather warning is information, not a cue to empty the shops. Read the **impact text** for your area, not only the colour.

## Warning colours (Met Office)

- **Yellow** — be aware; read which weather is meant.
- **Amber** — increased likelihood of disruption; plan and prepare.
- **Red** — dangerous weather; take action to protect life and property.

## Practical steps the day before and during

- Charge phones and power banks.
- Check travel plans; delay non-essential journeys if advised.
- If flooding is possible, check flood risk and move vehicles from known flood zones when safe.
- Review your [checklist](/checklist/) and paper contact numbers.
- Sign up for Met Office warnings if you have not already.

For flood messaging, use Environment Agency services in England and Wales, and SEPA Floodline in Scotland—do not mix the two systems.

You do not need new kit every time a warning appears. Fill genuine gaps over time.
