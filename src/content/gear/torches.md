UK guidance prefers torches over candles in a power cut. Check the torch you already own before buying another.

## How we compare

We prioritise usable indoor light, spareability, and whether claims are **desk research** or a **firsthand test**. Turbo lumen figures matter less than how long a torch runs at a comfortable household brightness.
